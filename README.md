# Interli Plural

This is the app for plural systems to encourage communication, discover roles and hobbies, tackle memory issues and truly get a hand (back) on your life. Even for people who are not plural, this app can come in handy and everyone is welcome to use it.


##  Features
- Plural
  - **Fronting / members tracking**: Keep track of who is at front, build profiles for each member and group them if you like.
  - **Subsystem tracking**: Keep track of who is in a subsystem and who's fronting within that subsystem.
  - **Who Am I?**: Blurry? Confused about the idenitity? Based on data from the Moodtracker and individual This Is Me data can help you figure out about who you are.
  - **Relationships**: make mindmaps about the relationships in and outside the system. It doesn't stop at one mindmap, you can make different tabs!
  - Communicaton:
    - **Sysmail**: Sending emails to members personally.
    - **Sysmedia**: An internal social media experience for your system (including DMs for private chatting)
- Health
  - **Physical health**: The body's physicial health is important, here you can track food, hydration, medication and other things.
  - **Mood Tracker**: Tracking how members and the body is doing, what they are doing and when they are doing, how do they feel about the activities and what patterns are recognized to make someone feel better or what happened that the body is so out of energy when memory has gone 'woosh'
- Administration:
  - **Notes**: Write notes or make journals.
  - **To Do**: Make to do lists or shoppinglists.
  - **Calendar**: Something happening in the outer? Something in the inner? You can register it.
- Statistics:
  - **Front Statistics**: Statistics based on fronting, duration, patterns and history
  - **Mood Statistics**: Statistics based on moods and activities for pattern/habit recognition, and view history.
  - **Member-Mood correlation**: View patterns from what members do which activities the most and how they generally feel about those activities. For tracking habits and talents.
- Settings:
  - **Layout**: Colour themes (switches along with member switches)
  - **App settings**: Changing the app starters page, enabling and disabling pages and modules, enabling and disabling certain statistic graphics, language Dutch/English, font size and manage notifications.
  - **Backup & Data**: Thirdparty import (plural apps, Daylio), export backups, import backups, set auto device backup, export data to PDF, restore achived/deleted members and delete all data (multiple tab safety).
  - **Members & Fields**: bulk move members (put a lot of members immediately in a group, or archive them), view archived members, make custom fields and templates for those fields, view member registration history an enabling or disabling to view subsystem fronts on the memberpage.
- Other things to note about the app:
  - **Organize global or per member?**: Both, the app is designed to register everything about the body and mind but yet you can view almost all data individually per member on their personal profiles, for example if you need to know if a certain headmate is alright or not or when they were around  for the last time.
  - **Customization**: Entirely customizable themes which you can even link to certain members which change along when a switch happen further the app has the ability to enable/disable pages you don't use to prevent overwhelm.
  - **Data Portability**: Export your data for backups, or as PDFs to share with therapists. Export notes to Word or PDF. Make sure to keep your backup somewhere save, the developer has 0 access to your data so if something happens to your device it is your responsibility to have put your data somewhere save.
  - **Privacy**: Local backups and offline approach.
  - **Deletion**: Almost everywhere in the app there is a 8x tap system to prevent accidental deletions.
  - **Frontnotifications**: A notification in the statusbar that shows who's currently registered as front.
  - **WORK IN PROGRESS, more will be added and fine-tuned over time. Developer listens actively to feedback from the community or requests (Discord)**

##  Technical Details
- **Platform**: Android (Min SDK 24)
- **Language**: Kotlin
- **markdown rendering**: Markwon
- **Key Libraries**: Coil (Images), Markwon (Markdown), WorkManager (Backups), Gson (Data).
